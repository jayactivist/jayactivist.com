Biography

Jay Activist is the definition of charisma. Raised on the Southside of Atlanta with Caribbean flavor, he blends moody trap and soulful vibes into music painting stories of late nights, complicated love, and the introspective thoughts of someone young navigating life.

Jay Activist first made his stamp on the rap scene going viral for his record “Still Atlanta” as a response to Omeretta and has since continued to grow his fanbase through college campuses across Atlanta with help from organizations such as NCEG. From the Sugar Honey Iced Tea EP through You Knew What This Was to his current single “Maybe I’m the Problem” (with a remix featuring Qui arriving Nov 24), Jay Activist isn’t just telling stories — he’s building a soundtrack for those who play the game with heart and style, with the new era We’re Just Friends (WJF) coming next.
