Bio

Jay Activist didn’t wait for a moment, he built one.

Coming out of the Southside of Atlanta, he started on college stages where crowds didn’t know his name yet. That didn’t last long. Open mics turned into packed rooms, and packed rooms turned into people showing up just to see what he was going to do next.

At the center of Jay’s music is storytelling. His catalog plays like an ongoing conversation about love, ambition, situationships, heartbreak, confidence, and the things people have trouble saying out loud. Titles like Lose You Again and MAYBE IM THE PROBLEM feel like chapters in a bigger story, each release connected to what came before while revealing something new.

His sound lives between R&B and rap, moving from smooth and melodic to introspective and conversational without losing his identity. Behind it all is an obsessive work ethic: recording, performing, testing ideas, studying what connects, and doing it again.
